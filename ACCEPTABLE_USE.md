# Acceptable Use

Zion is research-grade software released under the Apache License 2.0. The license grants you broad rights to use, modify, and distribute the software. This document describes how the project authors *hope* you use it. **None of it is legally binding beyond the Apache 2.0 license.**

## What Zion is for

- Defending applications, services, and infrastructure that you operate
- Sovereign edge deployments where regional or organizational signals matter
- Security research, both offensive and defensive
- Building admission control and traffic intelligence into systems that need them

## What we hope you do not do

- Discriminate against end users based on protected characteristics
- Use Zion to enforce mass censorship or surveillance of populations
- Suppress legitimate journalism, political speech, or dissent
- Weaponize sovereign tags as a "this country is bad" filter — Zion's classifications are descriptive (origin, sector), not pejorative
- Deploy Zion in transit paths where net-neutrality regulations apply (Reg. EU 2015/2120 art. 3) without ensuring your use falls within the security exception of art. 3(3)

## Provenance and attribution

This project carries a public record of its lineage. See [NOTICE](NOTICE) for the canonical attribution required by the Apache 2.0 license.

If you fork or rebrand Zion or its components, the NOTICE attribution is mandatory under the license. Beyond legal compliance, we ask that you reach out to the maintainers. Open dialogue is more useful than legal escalation, and the maintainers are easier to reach than you might expect.

## Reporting abuse

If you observe Zion being used in ways that contradict the spirit of this document, please open an issue or contact the maintainers privately via the channels listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) where applicable.

Zion is the defensive crystallization of red-team research. Use it to make the internet safer, not to make it smaller.
